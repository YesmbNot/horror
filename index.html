Station Bulletin · October 14, 1994

Until further notice WKVN will interrupt regular overnight programming at 3:33 AM to carry safety instructions from the Harlow Valley Office of Civil Readiness. Please keep your television tuned to Channel 9 and your volume at a level you can hear from your bedroom.

Residents are asked not to call the station. Our phone lines are for emergency use only. Several viewers have reported calls from the station that we did not place. WKVN does not call viewers.

— Ruth Calloway, Station Manager
